Capital One: AI Deal Summary Tool

How I delivered a digital AI product that reduced funding time by 55% and drove a projected $1.2M in annual savings.

Funders were spending the majority of their time hunting down context scattered across 6+ data sources before they could even begin evaluating an application. I led the discovery, scoping, and delivery of a generative AI summary tool that changed that.

🔍 The Problem

Funders were drowning in context-gathering

Before touching an application, funders had to manually pull together information from 6+ disconnected sources: documents, policies and procedures, notes, tasks, stipulations, and dealer messages. There was no single view of "what do I need to do to fund this deal?" The result was slow decisions, frustrated specialists, and a massive throughput bottleneck for the business.

🎙 Discovery

100+ interviews revealed the real blocker

I conducted over 100 interviews with funding specialists across experience levels. The pattern was clear: it wasn't that funders didn't know their jobs. It was that getting the context needed to do their job took too long. Every minute spent gathering information was a minute not spent funding. The insight that shaped everything: speed of context is the product.

⚡ The Solution

A generative AI deal summary that surfaces everything a funder needs the moment they open an application

I defined the product as a single-screen AI summary that ingested all deal data including documents, P&P data, notes, tasks, stipulations, and dealer messages, then generated a plain-language briefing showing outstanding tasks, next steps, and validation requirements. The goal was to get a funder from "opened app" to "ready to act" in seconds, not minutes.

Phase 1
Accuracy first
Started with a scrappy UI. Iterated with engineers on model accuracy and summary quality until funders said the context was trustworthy.
Phase 2
Speed of delivery
Once accuracy was validated, focused on how fast the summary loads on app open. Every second of latency was a problem worth solving.
Phase 3
Polish the UI
Only after the summary was reliable and fast did we invest in a clean, clear interface. Iterated with funders continuously throughout.
⚖️ Key Tradeoff

Accuracy before aesthetics

We made a deliberate call to ship with a scrappy UI rather than delay for polish. The bet was that a trustworthy summary in an ugly wrapper was more valuable than a beautiful tool funders couldn't rely on. That sequencing was validated. Funders adopted it faster because it was right, not because it looked good.
